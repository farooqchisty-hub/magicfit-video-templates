Roadmap to 200+ video templates

22 styles, 9 or 10 templates each, every template duration-adaptive (15 to 60 s) and written to the v2 voice standard. Claude owns concepts and review; GPT-6 Luna Pro does the bulk expansion, scripting loops and vision QA.

Decisions needed now: 200 (9 per style) or 220 (10 per style); go ahead with Phase A including about $20 for the 2 videos; Seedance 2.0 Mini as the stylized default; UGC as the Luna pilot style.

Phase A: Lock the foundation

Before any scale work, so 200 templates are not built on rules that will change.

  • A1. Finish 30 s validation: redo the anime and cinematic videos under the v2 voice standard (anime on Seedance 2.0 Mini, cinematic on treg Seedance 2.5 face with cast cards).
  • A2. Close the open learnings: re-render product-free location plates for all 22 templates with vision QA; worn-product persistence flag; sweep hand-contact phrasing; lint either/or options; ship product-free keyframes; per-shot caption position.
  • A3. Migrate the 22 flagships to spec v2: tiered beats for 15/30/45/60 s, narrator persona, sentence ranges, voice standard; every script through the gate; honest fit re-ratings.
  • A4. Set defaults in AGENT.md and tools: Seedance 2.0 Mini for stylized, treg Seedance 2.5 face for photoreal, 480p preview then 720p final.

Gate: you approve the 2 videos and the v2 flagships about $20 videos + $3 plates + $1 Luna

Phase B: Concepts for the full set (Checkpoint 4)

Claude owns this phase: it is where quality and diversity are decided.

  • B1. Diversity matrix per style: story engine, world, cast, tone, the ad's job, product-type coverage (at least 2 great per type per style), category coverage across the bundle, sub-looks (felt, crochet, noir, mecha and so on).
  • B2. About 15 candidate loglines per style, pruned to 9 or 10, with a similarity check so no two concepts in a style feel alike, each grounded in a proven pattern.
  • B3. One review page with all 200 to 220 loglines grouped by style, matrix visible.

Gate: you approve the concept list near zero

Phase C: Luna pilot on one style

Prove GPT-6 Luna Pro can expand concepts to the flagship bar before trusting it with 200.

  • C1. Luna Pro expands 10 approved UGC concepts into full v2 templates, with lint and script-gate retry loops.
  • C2. Render their assets and keyframes, Luna vision QA, contact sheets.
  • C3. Claude reviews every one against the flagship; tune the Luna brief from what fails.

Gate: you approve the pilot batch about $1 Luna + $8 images

Phase D: Production at scale

Batches of about 5 styles at a time.

  • D1. Luna Pro expands every approved concept to a v2 template (lint and gate loops until PASS).
  • D2. Assets per template: cast cards, location plates, signature props, product-free keyframes (Nano Banana Pro), Luna vision QA with re-rolls.
  • D3. Claude reviews each style (deep check of 1 in 5, fit ratings, diversity), publishes contact sheets and template pages.

Gate: you approve each batch of styles about $150 to $200 images + $15 Luna

Phase E: Validation videos

Real videos on random template and product pairs to catch what text checks cannot.

  • E1. 10 to 15 videos across styles and all 7 product types at 30 s on Mini, plus a few at 15 and 60 s.
  • E2. Fold every lesson into runner, style or template rules and re-lint all templates.

Gate: you approve the sample about $50 to $75

Phase F: Package and productize

  • F1. Final bundle: AGENT.md runner, tools, manifest, templates with assets; hosting on Cloudflare R2 with a Drive folder per template.
  • F2. One preview video per style (22) for the sales page.
  • F3. Bonus pack: the 69 MagicFit homepage videos converted to the same format.
  • F4. App path (optional): duration and product URL as inputs, preview-then-final flow, cost shown up front, pricing tiers.

Gate: you approve the package about $110 previews

Total estimate

About $350 to $450 in generation across all phases, most of it images and validation videos.
