Cinematic: Night Stint (single 30 s take)

Product: Ember Mug 2 · $14.25 all in

What worked
  • photoreal, same driver and crew chief throughout without any face reference
  • the mug is faithful: wordmark, glowing base ring, steam
  • her father's stopwatch pays off beside the mug at sunrise
Known issues
  • the model said the brand wrong in both takes; fixed by cloning her voice from her own lines and splicing 'Ember Mug' (both transcribers now agree), lips are close but not perfect
  • wardrobe drifted from the cast card (white suit, black polo) because Replicate blocks face references
  • a readable 79 on a pit sign and numbers on the tyres
  • the improvised radio chatter was muted in the edit
Biggest finding. Replicate's Seedance rejects photoreal human faces as references. Stylized templates work fully there. Photoreal templates need either a provider that accepts face references or the single 30 second take used for the Ember video, which keeps people consistent but makes the brand line a single point of failure (fixed here with a voice-clone splice).
